*The whole process of reprocessing has three mail blocks: SELENE, buddy-check and SELENEVIS. 
	*SELENE is a process that calls info from "/datafiles", the format is given in Workshop notes. We carry out qc() and qc2(). they read from a json in /configuration/stations.json and harmonics from /configuration/harmonics
		-qc
			-flatline (stuck value) test
			-range test (max-min)
			-spike detection
			
		-qc2(): advanced spike detection. when gradient surpasses a max2.
	We save results "[code2]_original_sampling_flags.out" in "/outputs". 

	*buddy-check reads from and produces results in "/outputs".  
		*compare to hourly surge of buddy station (nearby station of less than 0.1degree, we enlarge distance to 0.3, 0.5...) 
		*compare to monthly total sea level of buddy station
		also:
			*compare to altimetry. reads altimetry from /altimeter_data.
			*compare to 
			*check for attenuated data
			*check for drifts
		*changes are made on hourly data, and propagated to original sampling (can be 1min, 5min, etc. )
		*To produce hourly tide and surge, we call utils/utide_construct, which reads harmonics in /configuration/harmonics. 

		*produces [code2]_original_sampling_buddy.out, [code2]_hourly_slev_buddy.out, [code2]_hourly_surge_buddy.out, [code2]_hourly_tide_buddy.out
